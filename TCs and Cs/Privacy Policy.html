This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, processed, stored, used, shared, and protected when individuals access or use the application. The application aims to comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), where applicable, and other relevant South African legislation. 2. Who Is Responsible for Your Information? The organisation or person determining the purpose and means of processing personal information will generally be the responsible party under POPIA. Responsible party details: * Organisation: Sandstorm Team * Contact Email: bika.version1gmail.com * Information Officer: Neo Dikhutso The responsible party must fulfil the legal obligations applicable to its activities. 3. Information We May Collect Depending on the application’s features, we may collect: Account Information * Name or chosen username. * Email address. * Phone number. * Account credentials, stored securely. Reports and Support Requests * Details of incidents reported by users. * Dates, descriptions, and relevant circumstances. * Information about alleged perpetrators, witnesses, or affected individuals. Sensitive Information * Information relating to health, sexual violence, or other sensitive circumstances, where submitted and lawfully processed. * Supporting documents, photographs, audio recordings, or other evidence, where the application permits uploads. Technical Information * Device and application information. * Security logs. * IP addresses or diagnostic information, where collected. * Location information, only where the application uses such a feature and an appropriate lawful basis exists. The exact information collected will depend on the features used. We aim to minimise collection and will not request information that is unnecessary for the stated purpose. 4. Why We Process Personal Information Personal information may be processed for purposes such as: * Creating and managing user accounts. * Receiving and managing support requests or reports. * Connecting users with relevant support services. * Providing safety information and resources. * Maintaining application security. * Detecting misuse, fraud, or unauthorised access. * Responding to lawful requests. * Meeting legal and regulatory obligations. Information will not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis. 5. Lawful Processing Under POPIA Personal information will be processed in accordance with the conditions and requirements of POPIA. Depending on the circumstances, processing may rely on consent, a legal obligation, the performance of a contract, legitimate interests recognised by law, or another applicable lawful justification. Where consent is required, users will receive appropriate information about the processing and may withdraw consent, subject to applicable law. Information relating to health, sex life, alleged criminal conduct, or other specially protected categories requires particular care. The organisation must determine whether the relevant statutory conditions for processing such information are satisfied. Consent alone does not automatically make every form of sensitive-data processing lawful. 6. Sharing Personal Information Personal information will not be sold to third parties. Information may be shared where necessary and legally justified, including with: * Authorised employees or team members who require access to perform their duties. * Secure technology providers processing information on our behalf. * Support organisations where sharing is authorised and appropriate. * Public authorities where disclosure is legally required or otherwise lawfully justified. Third-party service providers handling personal information on our behalf must be subject to appropriate contractual confidentiality and security requirements. We will limit disclosures to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. We will not automatically send every report to the police or another organisation unless the application’s disclosed procedures and applicable law permit or require that action. 7. Protecting Personal Information We will take appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access, unlawful processing, alteration, and disclosure. Depending on the system, safeguards may include: * Encryption in transit and at rest. * Strong authentication and access controls. * Restricted staff permissions. * Secure backups. * Security testing and updates. * Staff confidentiality obligations. * Monitoring for suspicious activity. * Procedures for responding to security incidents. These measures must be implemented and maintained in practice. Merely including them in this policy does not establish that the application is secure. No online service can guarantee absolute security. 8. Security Breaches If a security compromise occurs, the responsible party will investigate and respond in accordance with applicable law. Where POPIA section 22 applies, the responsible party must notify the Information Regulator and affected data subjects as soon as reasonably possible after discovering a qualifying security compromise, subject to the statutory requirements and exceptions. Users will be provided with relevant information and guidance where required. 9. Data Retention and Deletion Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its lawful purpose, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. Retention periods will be determined according to the type of information and the reason it is held. When information is no longer lawfully required, it will be securely deleted, destroyed, or de-identified where appropriate. Deleting an account may not automatically delete records that must lawfully be retained. 10. Your Rights Under POPIA Subject to applicable law, users may have the right to: * Request access to their personal information. * Request correction or deletion of inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, outdated, incomplete, misleading, or unlawfully obtained information. * Object to certain forms of processing. * Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. * Request information about third parties who have received their personal information, where applicable. * Submit a complaint to the Information Regulator. Requests can be sent to bika.version1@gmail.com. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements. 11. Children’s Personal Information If the application may be used by children, the organisation must assess and comply with the additional requirements that apply to children’s personal information under POPIA. Appropriate age-related safeguards, consent mechanisms, and child-safety procedures must be established where necessary. The application will not knowingly process children’s personal information unlawfully. 12. International Data Transfers If personal information is transferred to or stored in another country, the organisation will assess and comply with the applicable requirements of POPIA, including section 72 where relevant. Appropriate legal and contractual safeguards will be implemented where required. 13. Cookies and Tracking If the application uses cookies, analytics tools, advertising identifiers, or similar technologies, users will be informed about the relevant practices. Where required, consent or another appropriate lawful basis will be obtained. The application will avoid unnecessary tracking, particularly where it could expose a survivor’s identity, behaviour, or location. 14.
