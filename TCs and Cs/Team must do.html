WHAT YOUR TEAM MUST DO TO STRENGTHEN LEGAL PROTECTION Having Terms and Conditions on your app is not the same as having legal protection. Your actual design, security practices, contracts, and handling of GBV reports matter just as much. Here is your team’s practical checklist. 1. Comply with POPIA The Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 is central to your project. Your team should: * Identify the legal entity or person responsible for personal information. * Determine whether you must register an Information Officer with the Information Regulator. * Document what information you collect and why. * Establish an appropriate lawful basis for each type of processing. * Assess whether your app processes special personal information or children’s personal information. * Create a retention and deletion policy. * Restrict access to reports and survivor information. * Establish procedures for handling privacy complaints and data breaches. * Put appropriate agreements in place with hosting providers and other service providers.
